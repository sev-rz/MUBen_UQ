# Round 2 (items C3 and C4): evidence-based prior and Laplace on every ensemble member; protocol FROZEN before any of these computations
Motivation. Round 1 (n0143) showed that one fitted scalar per task reproduces or exceeds most of the last-layer Laplace NLL gain, and that the prior strength alpha, chosen by validation NLL on a grid, often sits at an edge. Two open questions: (C3) does a principled, validation-free choice of alpha (the marginal likelihood, 'evidence') give the same gain? (C4) does Laplace still help when it is applied to every member of an ensemble?

## Common setting
Same saved networks as item B and round 1: scaffold split, eight datasets, DNN/ChemBERTa/GIN seeds 0-9, 100, 200 (12 networks), Uni-Mol seeds 0-9 (10 networks); the stage code of each backbone for features, Hessians (analytic, last layer, frozen noise head in regression) and prediction is unchanged; no retraining; the primary Laplace arm (alpha per task on validation NLL, grid 1e-4..1e4) is recomputed in the same pass and must reproduce the item-B per-network NLL (tolerance 1e-6) or be flagged. Test data are used only for the final metrics of arms fixed on training or validation data. Exploratory (test sets opened for baselines).

## C3: evidence-based prior (script evidence.py)
For each task the Laplace log marginal likelihood of the prior precision, up to terms independent of alpha, is E(alpha) = (D/2) log alpha - (alpha/2) ||theta||^2 - (1/2) sum_k log(lambda_k + alpha), where theta (D parameters including the bias) are the trained last-layer weights, lambda_k the eigenvalues of the data Hessian (training data only) and alpha the precision of the N(0, alpha^-1 I) prior. alpha_ev = argmax over the grid 10^-8 ... 10^4 (121 log-spaced values). No validation or test data are used to choose alpha_ev. Arms per network: Deterministic; Laplace primary; Laplace with alpha_ev; one-parameter recalibration (round 1 definition, validation-fitted).
Endpoints per network: d_ED = NLL(evidence) - NLL(Deterministic); d_EP = NLL(evidence) - NLL(primary). Rule R10 per dataset on d_ED and on d_EP (at least 90 % of networks negative and the 95 % t-interval below zero: 'better'; symmetric: 'worse'; otherwise 'unclear'); a backbone is 'improves' with R10 on at least 6 of 8 datasets; family sign-flip test over the eight dataset t-statistics. Also reported: the share of task fits with alpha_ev at a grid edge, the median of log10(alpha_ev) - log10(alpha_primary).
Hypotheses written in advance. H_C3a: alpha_ev is larger than the validation-tuned alpha on most task fits (median difference of log10 > 0), the posterior is therefore narrower, and the mean NLL of the evidence arm is worse than the validation-tuned arm by at least 0.02 (mean d_EP >= +0.02) on at least three of four backbones (training-data evidence of an overfit, early-stopped network favours a tight prior). H_C3b: the evidence arm still improves on Deterministic (R10 'better' on d_ED) on at least 4 of 8 datasets for at least two backbones.

## C4: Laplace on every ensemble member (script ens_laplace.py)
Ensemble = the ten Deterministic networks of seeds 0-9 (the pooled scaffold ensemble of the published design). Arms: E_D: the ten Deterministic predictions pooled as in the pipeline (means averaged; regression variances averaged; classification probabilities averaged); E_L: each member replaced by its own primary-Laplace prediction (alpha per task per member on validation), pooled in the same way; E_S: each member replaced by its own one-parameter recalibration (validation-fitted), pooled in the same way. E_D must reproduce the stored pooled-ensemble NLL (tolerance 1e-6) or be flagged.
Endpoint per dataset: the paired NLL difference E_L - E_D (and E_L - E_S, E_S - E_D) per molecule, macro over tasks as in check E; bootstrap over test molecules (2000 resamples, one resample matrix shared by all arms), z = estimate / bootstrap SE, verdict 'improves' if the 95 % percentile interval lies below zero, 'worsens' above, else 'unclear'. Family: exact one-sided sign-flip test over the eight dataset z-values per backbone, Holm over the four backbones. A backbone 'Laplace helps the ensemble' with 'improves' on at least 6 of 8 datasets.
Hypotheses written in advance. H_C4a: E_L improves on E_D on at least 6 of 8 datasets for at least three of four backbones (the pooled ensemble variance is an average of overconfident member variances, so Laplace still adds information). H_C4b: the mean NLL gain of E_L over E_D is smaller in magnitude than the mean single-network Laplace gain of item B (-0.077 DNN, -0.126 ChemBERTa, -0.144 GIN, -0.221 Uni-Mol) for at least three of four backbones.

## Decision rules and cost
Hypotheses are reported as supported or rejected against the thresholds above; nothing is tuned on test. Two Slurm jobs (Uni-Mol: about 70 min of GPU for the two scripts; the other backbones minutes). Outputs: results/<backbone>/<dataset>.json for each script, resumable per dataset.
