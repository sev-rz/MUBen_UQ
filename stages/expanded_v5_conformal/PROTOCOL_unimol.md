# Conformal prediction on Uni-Mol: protocol (FROZEN 2026-10-12, before any Uni-Mol conformal computation)
Same design as PROTOCOL_v2.md (sha256 in PROTOCOL_v2.sha256) applied to the Uni-Mol families, scaffold split only: families Uni-Mol (Deterministic, Temperature, MC dropout, SWAG, BBP, SGLD, Focal Loss, Ensembles; seeds 0-2, ensemble pooled) and Uni-Mol-Laplace (Laplace, Laplace constant, Novelty only, Novelty Laplace). Code: conformal_unimol.py = conformal_v2.py with the family filter, the Uni-Mol test path and the output folder changed; no change to scores, calibration or metrics.
Calibration set: the validation split of each run; coverage and size on the untouched test split; alpha 0.1 (primary) and 0.2; LAC and class-conditional LAC (classification), normalised and unnormalised score (regression); novel = maximum Tanimoto similarity (Morgan r2, 2048 bits) to the training set below 0.4; Mondrian groups with at least 20 calibration molecules.
Questions and rules as in PROTOCOL_v2: (Q2) does coverage hold under scaffold shift; (Q1/Q4) is a Laplace base narrower than Deterministic and ensembles at held coverage; contrasts Laplace minus Deterministic, Laplace minus ensembles, Novelty Laplace minus Laplace constant; a sign counts only if all seeds agree; a width comparison counts only where both methods hold coverage; an overall statement needs 6 of 8 interpretable datasets.
Hypothesis registered from the other three backbones: classification coverage of the Deterministic Uni-Mol on scaffold is at least 5 points below the 0.90 target (the other backbones: 0.75 to 0.82), regression coverage within 0.88 to 0.93; no contrast reaches 6 of 8. If Uni-Mol deviates this is reported as such.
Inputs are the saved validation and test predictions of the Uni-Mol reproduction (all methods after the extended evaluate stage) and of the Laplace stage; nothing is retrained.
