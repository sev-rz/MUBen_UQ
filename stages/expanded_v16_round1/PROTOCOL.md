# Round 1 (items C1, C2, C6): shift-aware conformal calibration for classification and regression, protocol FROZEN before any of these computations
Motivation. Split conformal coverage for classification falls to 0.75-0.82 (target 0.90) under scaffold shift while regression holds (n0089, report 07, check F). Check F showed that calibrating on test-internal scaffold groups restores 0.90 (upper bound, not deployable). Round 1 asks what a deployable correction can buy, using saved predictions only (no retraining, no new test predictions).

## Common setting
Scaffold split; backbones DNN, ChemBERTa, GIN (Uni-Mol: separate later step); methods Deterministic, Laplace, Ensembles; alpha 0.1 (target 0.90); LAC score (classification: 1 - p of the true label), normalised absolute-error score (regression); calibration set = the validation split of each run (as in n0089); per-task calibration, tasks with fewer than 20 observed labels or one class in the validation split skipped (as check F); evaluation requires at least 20 observed labels per task. Scaffold groups = Bemis-Murcko scaffolds of the test molecules (as check F). Aggregation as check F: per-task mean, per-dataset mean over tasks, mean over seeds, then over datasets within classification and regression.
Exploratory: test sets were opened for baselines; nothing here is tuned on test coverage (all arms are fixed below).

## C1: few labelled target molecules (the realistic version of check F)
For k in {0, 5, 10, 20, 50, 100}: 50 replicates; in each replicate a random order of the test scaffold groups is drawn (same order for all methods) and whole groups are moved into a labelled set until it holds at least k molecules; evaluation molecules are the molecules of the REMAINING groups (scaffold-disjoint from the labelled ones, as in check F). Arms: (a) pooled: validation scores plus the labelled scores, one set of weights; (b) balanced: weighted quantile with half of the mass on the validation scores and half on the labelled scores (k >= 5; total mass n/(n+1) plus a point mass 1/(n+1) at infinity, n = validation + labelled count); (c) target-only: labelled scores only, k >= 20. The k = 0 arm uses validation scores only and is evaluated on the same evaluation molecules of every replicate (paired).
Endpoints: coverage and size (interval width) ratio to k = 0, per k and arm. Hypothesis H_C1: for classification the balanced arm reaches mean coverage >= 0.88 at k <= 50 on at least two of three backbones; the pooled arm is slower. A backbone 'reaches' if the dataset-mean coverage is >= 0.88 at that k for Deterministic and Laplace.

## C2: similarity-weighted conformal prediction under covariate shift (Tibshirani et al. 2019 weights)
Weights w(x) = odds of 'test molecule' versus 'validation molecule' from an L2 logistic regression on 1024-bit Morgan (radius 2) bit vectors of the validation and test SMILES of the dataset (features only, no labels; class weights balanced; C = 1; probabilities clipped to [0.05, 0.95] so w lies in [0.053, 19]). Weighted quantile per test molecule x_j: calibration scores with weights w_i / (sum w + w(x_j)) and a point mass w(x_j) / (sum w + w(x_j)) at infinity; set or interval as in split conformal. Sensitivity arm: C = 0.1. Diagnostic: Kish effective sample size of the validation weights.
Endpoint: coverage and size ratio to unweighted validation calibration on all test molecules of each task. Hypothesis H_C2: weighting does NOT rescue classification coverage (mean coverage stays below 0.85 for at least two of three backbones), because a fingerprint classifier cannot see the model's error structure; a rise of >= 0.03 is reported as improvement.

## C6: conformal under the linear learning-rate schedule (ChemBERTa)
Validation predictions of the item-9 linear-schedule Deterministic and Laplace nets (seeds 0, 100, 200; from saved networks and posteriors; no retraining; the test split not touched) and their saved test predictions; same conformal recipe as n0089 (alpha 0.1; Deterministic and Laplace). Endpoint: scaffold coverage and size under linear versus constant schedule (classification and regression). Hypothesis H_C6: classification coverage stays below 0.88 under the linear schedule (the shift finding does not depend on the schedule).

## Decision rules written in advance
Every hypothesis is reported as supported or rejected against the stated threshold; 'reaches' and 'rescue' are judged on dataset-mean coverage, not on single datasets. A deployable correction is claimed only if it reaches >= 0.88 on at least two of three backbones for both Deterministic and Laplace.
